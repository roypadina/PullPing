# Changelog

All notable changes to PullPing are documented here.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

## [0.7.0] - 2026-11-15

> **Before you update:** going back to 0.6.1 afterwards resets PullPing's settings to their defaults and makes it sync your pull requests again from scratch (silently, without notifications). Accounts, automations and the activity log are kept; an older version still opens every automation in the same app, but once it saves a change, the Open With list is rebuilt from the apps in use (names and order lost) and your own scenario prompts are gone.

### Changed
- **Lists sort by whose turn it is.** The sidebar (and the menu-bar panel's filter) now has **Needs Me**, **My Pull Requests**, **Reviews**, **Issues**, **Watching**, **Closed** and **Muted & Hidden**, plus Activity. My Pull Requests splits into **Your Turn** (changes requested, CI failed, conflicts, or approved and ready to merge), **Waiting on Others** (reviewers, CI, auto-merge) and **Drafts**; Reviews into **Needs Your Review**, **Team Requests** and **Reviewed**; Issues into **Assigned to Me**, **Opened by Me**, **In My Repositories** and **Mentioned**. Needs Me gathers everything waiting on you in one list, oldest first, and is what the menu-bar number counts. Search covers every row. The Done group is gone: merged and closed items are under Closed, open ones stay where they belong.
- **Notifications only when something needs you.** A panel now appears only for a review or re-review asked of you, changes requested, CI failed or conflicts on your pull request, a mention, an assignment, a new issue in your repositories, new comments on your pull requests and issues, or your pull request becoming ready to merge. Merges, CI passing (unless it makes your pull request ready to merge), new commits, labels and the rest go to **Activity** without a notification. The panel's title says what is asked of you ("Re-review needed", "Ready to merge").
- **Settings → Notifications** has one row per reason (Off, Banner or Alert) instead of two 19-row tables. Your earlier choices carry over: each reason takes the stronger of the old styles behind it, and anything you had turned off stays off. Phone pushes are unchanged and still chosen per event.
- **Activity** is a read-only log: a filter button in the toolbar (All, Needs Me, My PRs, Reviews, Runs; filled while filtering), a **New** section for entries since your last visit (and a dot on the sidebar row), chips for Notified, Phone and runs, and Copy Link in the row menu.
- Rows show one status chip that says whose turn it is: "Review requested by @carol", "Re-review · 2 pushes since your review", "Changes requested by @carol", "Approved · Ready to merge", "Waiting on @alice, @bob", "You approved · updated since"…
- Review reminders also cover re-reviews, and team requests while their notification is on.
- **What the agent is asked depends on what the pull request needs from you.** The default Handle with Agent prompt (and a new rule's) ends with the task of its scenario: review requested, re-review, changes requested, new comments or a mention, CI failed, conflicts, ready to merge, assigned, or anything else. The built-in tasks ask the agent to explain and propose, then wait for your OK (for a review: draft it, don't post it), instead of "work out what it needs and do it". A prompt you edited is kept as it is; add `{task}` to it to use the scenario's task.
- The pull request's right-click menu has **Handle with Agent ▸** with all its choices; **Run Automation ▸** keeps rules and hooks. The handler editor no longer has Open in (it uses the default under Open With), and a rule's Open in picks one of the Open With entries.
- `--launcher` on `pullping rule|hook|handler` picks (or adds) the matching Open With entry; `pullping handler set --launcher` makes it the default.
- Ghostex: a session PullPing starts there comes to the front. Resuming a session that is already open in Ghostex focuses it instead of starting a second copy, and types one line into its input (without pressing Return) that points at the prompt; `pullping session resume` does the same instead of refusing. An automatic run never types into an open session: it asks with a **Go to Session** button instead.
- **Selecting a pull request in the window marks it read at once** (it was after 1 second). Settings → General → Inbox → **Mark as read when selected**: Immediately, after 1, 2 or 5 seconds, or Never. In Needs Me the selected row now stays in place until you move on, even when reading it ended its reason (a mention, new comments, a reminder).
- **Snooze** is **Snooze Notifications** in the menus (the panel button still says Snooze…), so it reads apart from Remind Me. Needs Me's third section **Mentions & Assigned** is now **Mentions, Assigned & Reminders**, and Needs Me gains **Issues in My Repositories**.
- **Interface polish.** A new **Settings → Menu Bar** tab holds the icon, its count and dot (General no longer scrolls); Settings → Automations lists Rules right after Handle with Agent and folds the scenario prompts into one row; removing a rule asks first; the panel header gives the breakdown its own full-width lines; the detail pane keeps Open in Browser and Mark as Read and puts Mute, Hide, Snooze and Stop Tracking under **⋯**; **Run Automation ▸** is also in the Pull Request menu; collapsed groups show a chevron in the panel too.
- New **Settings → Repositories** tab: Track Issues, Track Issues in My Repositories and Other Repositories moved there from General.

### Added
- **Select several pull requests at once** (⌘-click, ⇧-click, ⇧↑/⇧↓, ⌘A) in the window and the menu-bar panel; every row action and the **Pull Request** menu act on the whole selection. With two or more selected the detail pane shows "N Items Selected", what they need, and Mark as Read, Open in Browser and More. A multi-selection is never marked read by itself, and opening more than 10 in the browser asks first.
- **Mark as Unread.** Mark as Read turns into **Mark as Unread** (⇧⌘U, the same command) once an item is read: its dot comes back, and if a mention, a comment on your pull request or a reminder had put it in Needs Me, it is back there and in the count.
- **Remind Me ▸** (row menu, Pull Request menu, the detail's ⋯ menu): in 1 hour, in 3 hours, tomorrow at 9:00, next week at 9:00, or **Custom…** (⌃⌘R) for any date and time. A row shows "Remind Tomorrow, 9:00 AM" until then. When it is due the item becomes new and needs you again: a "PullPing reminded you" entry in its activity and in Activity, a **Reminder** chip, a place in Needs Me (and the count), an Alert panel titled **Reminder**, and the phone if you tick **Reminder** in Settings → Phone. Reading the item ends it. Unlike Snooze, which keeps an item quiet until a time and does nothing then, a reminder brings it back to you. Mute still silences the panel.
- **Settings → Shortcuts** lists every shortcut. New menu shortcuts **⌃⌘M** Mute, **⌃⌘H** Hide and **⌃⌘R** Remind Me ▸ Custom…, and **single keys** while a pull request list has focus (window and panel): **O** open, **U** read/unread, **M** mute, **H** hide, **R** Remind Me (one switch turns them off). They work on non-Latin keyboard layouts too. To change a menu shortcut, use System Settings → Keyboard → Keyboard Shortcuts → App Shortcuts.
- **Numbers and dots.** Every account segment, filter (also inside the panel's filter menu) and sidebar row shows how many items need you (the number) and a dot when something there happened that you have not opened yet: a comment, a review, CI, a merge or a new request, by someone else. The menu-bar icon's dot shows while anything is new (Settings → Menu Bar → **Show a dot for new activity**, on by default); the panel header says how many ("2 new"); a collapsed account, Drafts, Waiting on Others or Reviewed shows a dot when it holds something new. A dot goes when you open the item, select it in the window or mark it read, not when you open the panel; muted, hidden and snoozed items light no group dot. Numbers inside the app now always count what needs you, also when the menu-bar count is All unread or Off.
- **Background runs and drafts** (off by default: Settings → Automations → Background Runs, `pullping background on`). **Review in Background** and **Summarize** in each pull request's Handle with Agent menu, and rules with `--background review|summarize`, start Claude Code without a terminal (Codex: not yet, a run is refused; it still opens in a terminal). It only reads: PullPing fetches the diff and discussion first, and the agent gets read-only file tools (`--restricted --safe-mode --tools Read,Grep,Glob`, no MCP, no CLAUDE.md; needs Claude Code 2.1.248 or later). The run ends in a draft: **Review draft ready** with Review Draft…, Open Session and Discard. In the draft window you edit the review and choose Comment, Request changes or Approve (Comment is preselected when the diff was too large to send whole, and the window says how much the agent saw); **Post** sends that one review with the account's token, pinned to the reviewed commit. This is the first time PullPing writes to GitHub, and only on that click: approvals and change requests are never automatic, no setting or command posts, and the first post per account asks once. 2 runs at once, 20 a day, 10 minutes each. `pullping background run|list|cancel|status`, `pullping drafts list|show|discard`.
- **New issues in your repositories.** PullPing now also tracks every open issue in the repositories your accounts own (Settings → Repositories → **Track Issues in My Repositories**, on by default; **Other Repositories** adds `owner/name` or `owner/*`). A new issue pops a **New issue** notification, counts in Needs Me (**to triage**) until you read it, and stays under **Issues ▸ In My Repositories** while it is open. Issues that were already open are listed without a notification. Replies on those issues notify like comments on your own.
- **Agents on issues.** New event **New issue** (`issueOpened`, `pullping kinds`) for rules, hooks and Handle with Agent; a **New issue** scenario prompt (`pullping prompts show issueTriage`); **Triage in Background** (and `--background triage`) drafts a reply you can edit and post as a comment from the draft window, below the kind of issue and the suggested labels. Labels and closing stay suggestions.
- **Mute a repository.** Right-click a pull request or issue ▸ **Mute owner/name** (also in the Pull Request menu): everything in that repository, new items too, stays listed but never notifies, never counts and never starts an automation on its own. Unmute it the same way or in Settings → Repositories; Edit ▸ Undo reverses it. One item can still be unmuted on its own.
- **Excluded Repositories** (Settings → Repositories): leave repositories out of Issues in My Repositories.
- **Repository filter.** The main window's toolbar (and View ▸ Repository) and the menu-bar panel's filter menu can show one repository only. Counts and notifications do not change.
- **Let your agent set it up.** Settings → Automations → **Copy Setup Prompt** (or `pullping setup prompt`): paste it into Claude Code or Codex and it walks you through every option, one question at a time, following the guide inside your copy of PullPing (`pullping setup guide`, also [docs/agent-setup.md](docs/agent-setup.md)). It never turns on automatic runs or the search through your agent history without your yes. `pullping setup status` shows what is set.
- **Open With.** Settings → Automations → Open With (`pullping targets`): up to 5 places where agent sessions open (Terminal, iTerm2, Ghostty, Ghostex or your own command line); the first is the default. Notification buttons use it; a new chevron next to them, the pull request's **Handle with Agent ▸** menu, the detail toolbar and the **Pull Request** menu offer every place. In Ghostex, focusing a session that already runs there is a setting of the entry. Rules can pick one (`--target`).
- **Resume Without Prompt** in those menus and `pullping session resume --no-prompt`: continue the linked session without sending it anything.
- **Prompts.** Settings → Automations → Prompts (`pullping prompts`) shows the task text of each scenario and lets you write your own, with a preview of the whole prompt. New placeholders `{task}`, `{scenario}`, `{scenario_label}`, `{resume_note}`, `{event_url}` and `{reviewed_sha}`.
- `pullping handler run --pr <ref> --dry-run` prints what a Handle with Agent click would start; `pullping run --scenario <id>`; `pullping --version`.
- **Re-reviews.** A pull request you reviewed is back in Needs Me when you are asked again, or when you requested changes (or your approval was dismissed) and the author pushed since, even without a new request.
- New reasons **to re-review** and **with conflicts** in Settings → Menu Bar → Count in the Menu Bar, and a **Conflicts** event (`pullping kinds`) when your pull request starts conflicting with its base branch.
- **Ready to merge** event (`pullping kinds`) when your pull request becomes mergeable after GitHub finishes checking it, so the notification no longer goes missing when CI passed before the merge check was done.
- A one-time note after the update explains the new lists and notifications.
- **Ko-fi support card.** Once in a while, after a pull request of yours is merged or approved, a small receipt card asks for a tip. It shows about 10 seconds after you close the menu-bar panel on a calm look: no account error, no review asked of you, nothing to fix on your pull requests, no reminder due, and in the last hour no failed CI or change request on yours and no failed run. It never shows before PullPing has pinged you more than 50 updates (counted from the version that added this card) and at least 7 days have passed, and at most 3 times a year. "Ask me in 2 weeks" snoozes it, "Nope, don't ask again" keeps it away until the next major version, and a tip keeps it quiet for 6 months. A new notification panel, a PullPing window, an update alert, sleep or a screen lock closes it as a snooze; so does leaving it unanswered for 5 minutes, or stepping away. It never takes keyboard focus: the same three answers are at the top of the right-click menu while it shows. Hold ⌥ in that menu for **Show Support Prompt Now**, a preview that changes nothing. The counts stay on your Mac.

### Fixed
- Opening Activity no longer crashes PullPing when it trims entries older than the keep period.
- The menu-bar panel closes when you click anywhere else (another app, the desktop, another menu-bar icon), press Escape, or switch apps.
- Menus in the menu-bar panel (the filter, ⋯) open on the first click instead of closing at once, and the filter menu shows each group's number and a dot for new activity.
- Settings, the main window and Check for Updates… open in front of the current app instead of behind it.
- Ghostex shows up under **Open in** again: Ghostex 11.5 changed its app id, so PullPing thought it was not installed and every Ghostex run failed.
- Runs in Ghostex start for real: since Ghostex 11.5 `ghostex terminal` only added a session that never started, so Resume Session and Handle with Agent looked successful and nothing ran. PullPing now uses `ghostex create-session --start` in the Ghostex project that holds the folder.
- A run that doesn't start (app not installed, folder missing, Handle with Agent off, no linked session, limits reached) now says why in a notification panel instead of only beeping; Recent Runs and Activity keep the detail.

## [0.6.0] - 2026-11-04

### Added
- Updates from inside the app. PullPing checks for a new version once a day and asks before installing it; tick "Automatically download and install updates" in that prompt to skip the question next time. The check downloads the release feed from GitHub and sends nothing about you. Check by hand with **Check for Updates…** in the About window. Updates are signed, and installing one this way needs no `xattr` step.

## [0.5.0] - 2026-11-02

### Added
- **Agent automations.** PullPing can start Claude Code or Codex in a terminal (Terminal, iTerm2, Ghostty or Ghostex, whichever are installed, or your own command line; `pullping launchers`) when a pull request event arrives. Write rules in the new **Settings → Automations** tab: the kinds of event, Any / Mine / Others, optional repositories and logins, a prompt with placeholders, a working directory, the agent (with an optional wrapper command) and where it opens. **Try with Latest Pull Request** checks a rule right away; **Recent Runs** shows what started and what failed.
- **Agent hooks and the `pullping` command.** `pullping hook add --pr … --on … --session …` lets an agent ask to be resumed on one pull request or issue, for example when it is approved or commented on. Agents get everything the Automations tab does: `pullping rule` and `pullping hook` `add`, `edit`, `show`, `list`, `enable`, `disable` and `rm`, `pullping run` (Run Automation, with `--dry-run`), `pullping runs` (Recent Runs), `pullping automations on|off|status` and `pullping kinds`; every reader and writer takes `--json` in the shape `automations.json` stores. The app picks changes up within 30 seconds, also when it was closed. Automations written from the command line are marked **CLI** in Settings, and the first time one is set to run automatically a notification says so. The command is the app binary itself, so the Homebrew cask links it as `pullping`; any other word after it is a command, never a second copy of the app. A Claude Code skill with the recipes is in `docs/claude-skill/pullping`.
- **Handle with Agent.** Turn it on in Settings → Automations (or `pullping handler on`) and the notifications you pick (Edit…, or `pullping handler set --on <kind,…|all>`; by default the ones an agent can act on, not CI passed, new commits, merges, closes, drafts, title or label changes), and the pull request's menu, get a **Handle with Agent** button: one click starts your agent on that pull request or issue with a prompt you can edit, in the agent, terminal and folder you pick. When the pull request has a linked session the button becomes **Resume Session** and **New Session**. It never runs on its own.
- **Linked sessions.** An agent links its session to a pull request with `pullping session set --pr <url> --session "$CLAUDE_CODE_SESSION_ID" --quiet` (also `hook add --session`), and every new Claude Code session PullPing starts on a pull request links itself. The link keeps the folder the session was started in, read from its transcript, so Resume Session finds it. A rule can resume the linked session (**The pull request's linked session**, `--session linked`). Links show in Settings → Automations → Linked Sessions and the main window's detail (`pullping session list|show|rm`) and expire after the pull request is merged or closed, or after 30 days.
- **Repository folders.** The working directory defaults to `{repo_dir}`: the repository's folder under Settings → Automations → Repository Folders (default `~/Code`; add as many as you keep repositories in), else a fallback folder (default `~`), so a run never fails only because a repository isn't cloned. `pullping automations folders list|add|rm` and `pullping automations fallback`. A failed Try shows its whole message, with the folder it tried.
- **Repository locator.** `{repo_dir}` is now the clone whose git remote is the repository (`owner/repo`, any host or SSH alias), found up to 3 levels below the repository folders whatever the clone is called (`~/Code/<org>/<repo>`, `~/src/github.com/<owner>/<repo>`, a renamed clone), then a folder named like the repository, then the fallback. A pull request's linked or earlier session's folder wins when it is inside a clone of the repository, and **Repository Overrides** (`pullping automations repos list|set|rm`) win over everything. `pullping automations folders list` shows how many clones PullPing finds. PullPing reads git's own files and never creates worktrees.
- **Worktree-aware folders.** New placeholders `{branch}` (the pull request's head branch, GitHub text: prompts only), `{worktree_dir}` (the worktree with that branch checked out, else the main worktree), `{main_dir}` and `{repo_root}` (the repository folder that holds the clone). Handle with Agent's default prompt names the worktree. Resume Session whose folder is gone starts in `{worktree_dir}` (Claude Code 2.1.223 and Codex resume by id from anywhere) and says so in Recent Runs; one whose transcript was deleted fails with "The session's transcript is gone".
- **Earlier sessions.** With no linked session, PullPing can find the local Claude Code or Codex session that worked on a pull request (the record Claude writes when a session creates it, its URL, or its branch), within the last 60 days, 1.5 GB or 10 seconds of reading; nothing leaves your Mac. **Look for earlier sessions** under Handle with Agent (off by default) turns Handle with Agent into **Resume Earlier** and **New Session** when one is found; **Find Earlier Session…** in the pull request's menu searches now. Agents use `pullping session find --pr <url> --link` and hand off with `pullping session resume --pr <url>`, which refuses a session that looks open in another terminal, the calling session itself and a second hand-off within a minute, and records who handed off; the default prompts suggest it.
- **Activity.** **Activity** in the main window's sidebar (for All Accounts and each account; also in the menu-bar icon's menu) lists everything PullPing saw and did: every event, whether it showed a banner or an alert, went to your phone, or was not shown and why (your own, notifications off, paused, muted, snoozed, off in Settings), review reminders, and every automation run and Handle with Agent launch. Newest first by day, with search; a row shows its pull request, and opens it in the browser even once it is no longer tracked. Kept 30 days (Settings → General → **Keep activity for**: 7, 30 or 90 days), at most 5000 entries, in `activity.jsonl`; `pullping activity [--since <time>] [--pr <ref>] [--json]` reads it while the app is closed.
- **Run buttons.** An automation asks first by default: its notification gets a **Run …** button and stays until you act, and the pull request's right-click menu and the main window's toolbar get **Run Automation** (`pullping run` from the command line). **Run automatically** is a per-automation choice. The main window's detail lists a pull request's agent hooks.
- Safety limits: no comment bodies or other GitHub text in any shell command or working directory, a prompt the agent never reads as an option, never a permission-bypass flag (the only argument PullPing adds is `--session-id` with a UUID it generated), your own comments and reviews (and pushes GitHub links to your account) never trigger anything, new commits after an automation ran on a pull request only offer it again, and each automation is capped per event, per pull request, per failed start and per check. A Run button left on a notification does nothing once its automation is turned off, removed or expired.

### Changed
- Nothing in the state file or the existing settings changes format (a new pull request's first events gain one optional field): a 0.4.0 build still opens everything this version writes and ignores the new `automations.json` and `activity.jsonl`.
- An open rule or Handle with Agent editor no longer overwrites a change made from the command line meanwhile: Save says "Changed elsewhere" and offers **Reload** or **Save Anyway**, and the Enabled switches and Repository Folders rows change only their own value.

### Fixed
- Accounts added with **Sign in with GitHub** no longer show "Token rejected" every day. GitHub sign-in tokens expire after 8 hours; PullPing now keeps the refresh token GitHub sends with them (in your login keychain) and renews the token by itself; a network drop during the renewal shows Offline (not "Token rejected") and it renews on the next poll, and a renewed token that cannot be saved to the keychain is reported on the account. Accounts signed in with an earlier version have no refresh token yet: sign in with GitHub once more (Add Account…, then remove the old entry). Personal access tokens are not affected.
- Agents PullPing starts are fresh sessions even when PullPing itself was opened from a terminal agent: the launch no longer passes on that agent's session variables (`CLAUDE*`, `CODEX_*` and those of a few session-switching tools); your shell profile still sets anything you export on purpose.

## [0.4.0] - 2026-10-30

### Added
- **Issues.** Open issues you opened, are assigned to, are mentioned in or commented on are tracked next to your pull requests, with an **Issue** chip on their rows. Comments, mentions, assignments, renames, reopening and closing notify like pull request events; a closed issue shows **Completed** or **Not planned**. Settings → General → Track Issues turns them off and removes them from the lists. Issues you were already involved in arrive quietly, marked read.
- **Label changes** on pull requests and issues: a new **Labels changed** kind in Settings → Notifications and Phone (banner on yours, off for others by default).
- **Assigned to me** in Settings → General → Count in the Menu Bar: an unread assignment, on a pull request or an issue, counts toward the menu-bar number. New comments on an issue assigned to you count as new comments.

### Changed
- The Notifications and Phone tables call their first column **Mine**: pull requests and issues you opened.
- Upgrade only: a 0.3.0 build cannot read state or settings written by this version (state moves to state.broken.json, settings reset).

## [0.3.0] - 2026-10-30

### Changed
- Lists are sorted by what needs you: pull requests waiting on you first, then ones with unread activity, then the most recently active. The panel, the main window and every filter use the same order.
- Pull requests with nothing left for you move to a **Done** group at the bottom: merged or closed ones, and others' pull requests you're only involved in once everything is read and no review is asked of you or your team. In the panel Done is collapsed, like Hidden; in the main window it's a section you can collapse, and PullPing remembers your choice.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-29

First release.

### Added
- Tracks every open pull request you are involved in, across any number of GitHub accounts: ones you opened, ones assigned to you, ones where your review is requested (directly or through a team), and ones where you commented, reviewed or were mentioned. Merged and closed pull requests stay listed for a few days (1, 3, 7 or 14, your choice).
- Menu-bar panel with your pull requests in three groups (Mine, To Review, Involved), each row showing CI status, review state, draft, merged or closed, and an unread dot. With several accounts, switch between All and each account; a filter menu picks Inbox, All, Mine, To Review, Involved, Recently Closed, Muted or Hidden. Both are remembered.
- Menu-bar count of the pull requests that need you (review requests, unread mentions, and your own pull requests with changes requested, failed CI, new comments, or ready to merge), spelled out in the panel header and the icon's VoiceOver label. Settings → General can count everything unread instead, or show no number.
- Right-click (or Control-click) the menu-bar icon: Open PullPing, Refresh Now, Mark All as Read, Dismiss All Notifications, Pause Notifications (1 hour, 3 hours, until tomorrow 9:00), Settings, About, Ko-fi and Quit.
- Main window with a Mail-style sidebar: the same filters for All Accounts and for each account (collapsible), with counts, plus search and a detail view with reviewers, checks and the recent activity of each pull request. Settings → General → Inbox switches to one inbox per account.
- Phone notifications through ntfy (Settings → Phone): server, topic, optional access token and fallback topic URL (both in the keychain), a test button, and a choice per kind of event for your own pull requests and for others, independent of the desktop style.
- Notifications for comments, mentions, reviews (approved, changes requested, commented), review requests, assignments, new commits, CI passed or failed, ready for review, converted to draft, merged, closed, reopened, dismissed reviews and title changes. Your own actions never notify you.
- Notification panels that appear at the top right without taking keyboard focus, one per pull request, with a choice of Off, Banner (hides itself) or Alert (stays) for each kind of event, separately for your own pull requests and the ones you review or follow. Open, Snooze (15 minutes, 1 hour, 3 hours, tomorrow 9:00), Mute and Dismiss are one click away; an optional sound plays when a panel appears.
- Review reminders: while your review is requested on an open, non-draft pull request, PullPing reminds you again every 15 to 240 minutes (every 60 by default) until you review it, the request is removed, or it is merged or closed.
- Mark as read, mute (keeps the row, silences panels and reminders), hide (leaves every list and count, still notifies), snooze, and Hide and Stop Tracking with Undo.
- Accounts with personal access tokens (classic or fine-grained), checked when you add them, with a clear status when a token is rejected, lacks a scope, needs SSO approval or hits the rate limit. "Sign in with GitHub" (device flow) appears once an OAuth client id is configured. Tokens live in your login keychain.
- Settings for how often to check GitHub (1 to 15 minutes, 3 by default), how long to keep merged and closed pull requests, launch at login, and showing the menu-bar icon.
- `pullping://` links: `open`, `open?pr=<id>`, `refresh` and `settings`, for Shortcuts and Raycast.
- Welcome and About windows, with a link to support the project on Ko-fi.
- Universal app (Apple Silicon and Intel) for macOS 14 or later.
